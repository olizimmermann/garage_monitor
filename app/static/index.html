AI Second Opinion (optional, Ollama)

A local vision model checks the camera frame when SSIM can't decide — e.g. after the scene changed or at night. It may always turn a reading into open, but only reports closed when SSIM itself was unsure.

Only models with vision support can see the camera. Smaller is faster: a 2B model answers in ~2 s on a Jetson Orin Nano.
